Trailmind Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 21, 2026

Plain-language summary: Your browsing memory lives encrypted on your own device. Trailmind does not show ads, does not sell your data, and never trains AI models on your browsing history. With cloud options off (the default), zero bytes of page content leave your browser.

1. Single Purpose & Chrome Web Store Limited Use Disclosure

Trailmind has a single clear purpose: providing a private, personal browsing memory inside your browser. The use and transfer to any other app of information received from Chrome APIs (including browsing history and bookmarks) adheres strictly to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

2. What Data Is Stored Locally

  • Readable text snapshots, extracted summaries, topics, entities, facts, and local embeddings stored inside Chrome IndexedDB encrypted at rest with AES-GCM 256 (WebCrypto).
  • Page visit timestamps, active reading time, and scroll depth for journeys and open loops.
  • Local routing logs (job type, duration, on-device vs. cloud count) with zero raw page content.

3. What Trailmind Never Collects

  • Incognito browsing windows or browser internal pages (chrome://, about:).
  • Passwords, typed form field values (input.value, textarea.value), or payment card information.
  • Excluded domains (pre-filled with banks, email providers, health portals, government ID portals, and password managers) or pages detected on-device as sensitive.

4. Hybrid AI & Optional Cloud Services

Per-page extraction, sensitive-page classification, and live Memory Radar are hard-locked to ONLY_ON_DEVICE via Chrome Gemini Nano. Optional cloud features (Ask Cloud, Decision Matrix cloud escalation, stats-only Weekly Recap polish, and PDF/audio analysis) are disabled by default. When explicitly enabled by you, only the minimum required text (retrieved passages, extracted facts, or aggregated stats) is sent via Firebase AI Logic protected by Firebase App Check, and every request is auditable in the Routing Inspector.

5. Optional End-to-End Encrypted Backup & Sync

If you opt in to Google sign-in for backup or sync via Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore, all records are encrypted on your device using a key derived from your passphrase (PBKDF2-SHA256, 600,000 iterations) before upload. Trailmind and Firebase never receive your passphrase or decryption key. You may delete all synced server data in one click in Settings.

6. Your Rights (GDPR & Nigeria NDPA)

  • Access & Portability: Export your complete memory as Obsidian Markdown ZIP or full JSON.
  • Erasure (Forget): Immediately delete the last 15 minutes, last 1 hour, any page, site, topic, cluster, date range, or your entire local database.
  • Withdraw Consent: Pause recording globally or per tab, disable cloud switches, or delete server sync records.

7. Children's Privacy

Trailmind is not directed at children under the age of 13 and does not knowingly collect data from children under 13.
